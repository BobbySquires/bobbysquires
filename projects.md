# Fed & Fiscal Challenge
I led Northeastern's teams for the Fed & Fiscal Challenges in 2024 and 2025. We placed 3rd nationally in the 2024 Fiscal Challenge and 3rd in the Boston region for the 2025 Fed Challenge. Recordings of our presentations are available on the [team's site](https://fedfiscalchallenge.sites.northeastern.edu/). [Linked here](https://github.com/BobbySquires/NEUFed2025) is a GitHub repository with graphs I made for the 2025 Fed Challenge.

# 2026 NEU Math Department REU
I participated in the Northeastern Math Department's 2026 Research Experience for Undergraduates (REU). I worked on a project investigating the burning numbers of different families of vertex-transitive graphs. I proved a partial result for the burning numbers of Kneser graphs. Linked here is our group's [final paper](https://drive.google.com/file/d/11i1OD_CbiKLmNAs9Jt20Q7Kp1dPjZvWz/view?usp=sharing) and [presentation](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1mXCvYTDb8-SJ0X5S3iIEfeDGocNaHGKm/view?usp=sharing).
